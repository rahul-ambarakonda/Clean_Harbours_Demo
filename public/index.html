Frequently asked questions.

What does Beacon do?
Beacon is an uptime monitor. It checks if your website or API endpoint is responding from multiple geographic locations. If it detects a problem, it alerts you immediately.
How often does Beacon check my site?
Checks are performed every 30 seconds for Team and Business plans, and every 60 seconds for the Solo plan. These checks originate from diverse global regions to ensure comprehensive coverage.
What kind of alerts does Beacon send?
For Team and Business plans, Beacon sends SMS alerts first, followed by a phone call if the alert is not acknowledged. Email is provided as a courtesy copy for all plans.
Can I get a status page?
Yes, Business plans include a public status page on your own domain, automatically updated when a check fails. This helps you communicate incidents to your users without manual updates.
What if I have more questions?
You can reach us at support@beacon.example. We are happy to answer any further questions you may have about Beacon.
